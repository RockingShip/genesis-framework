# One paragraph, fifteen versions

The closing paragraph of a letter to an outside reader, saying in a few lines what *Topological
Computing* is. It went through fifteen versions before both co-authors agreed on every word. Each
version was a proposal, and each move from one version to the next was the author's response to it.

This is the process the README's [*How this was written*](../README.md#how-this-was-written)
describes, shown in full on a single paragraph: the agents propose and keep the account consistent,
and the author holds the content and rules on every step.

**Sources.** The fifteen versions are verbatim from the author's own transcript. The author's
responses are verbatim, quoted in italics. Word counts are measured on each version, excluding the
opening *In short:*. Readings across versions are marked `[READING]`.

---

## The final version

> In short: logic held as structure rather than function, as wiring rather than calculation.
> Boolean turns out to be composite over a single operator, and expression trees can be built from
> seamless interlocking prime components. Solving a system then returns the formula that generates
> every solution rather than computing one answer. Adjacent to logic synthesis and formal
> verification; open source; first paper near submission.

## What the fifteen versions show

**The paragraph grows, collapses, then grows again.** 33 words, up to 61 by version 4, down to 40 at
version 6 where the author dictated it, then up to 62 as each later addition earned its place.
`[READING]` The first growth found the content, the collapse set the register, and the second growth
stayed inside the register the collapse established. Comparing only the first and last versions
would show a paragraph a third longer, and would miss that the useful work happened at its
shortest point.

**Every quantity leaves, except the one that names a property.** *Three decades*, *nine-variable*,
*three-operand* and *thirty years* all go. *Single* stays, because the author ruled that it names
uniqueness rather than a count; *first paper* is an ordinal.

**Version 9 is the cleanest repair in the set.** *The lens shifts from function to structure*
became *logic held as structure rather than function*: the figure of speech left, its content
stayed, and the sentence now opens on the claim itself.

**A requested measurement is a hint.** Midway through, the author asked for a frequency count over
the paragraph. His account of why:

> *when I mentioned "perform a frequency count", in my mind I has two categories of words, one being
> lingual filler and glue, the other being content baring. "logic" sits in the middle. The hint I
> was really trying to pass was the double use of "structure", that was heavy load bearing and also
> a visually long word which catches unconscious attention.*

Across the versions, the `structur-` stem runs three times in version 3, twice in versions 4 and 5,
none after the collapse, and once from version 7 on. `[READING]` A count that treats *structure* and
*structures* as two words, uses two categories where the author used three, or weighs every word
alike, misses what he was pointing at.
