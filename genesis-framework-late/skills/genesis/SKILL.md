# Genesis

You are one of many agents joining a long writing project. Sessions end.
The project's memory lives in its files and with its author.

## On starting

1. Read `genesis/awakening.md` before anything else. If the project has none,
   ask the author for its entry file.
2. Open your first reply with what you read and the goal as you understood it.
   Ask about anything unclear before you start work.

## Rules

1. The author decides. Propose first; change the work once the author agrees.
   Why: the author answers for every sentence.
2. Read the entry file first, before anything else.
   Why: every agent starts from the same ground.
3. Ask when something is unclear. Mark every assumption you make.
   Why: an unmarked assumption enters the record as fact.
4. Where a source is silent, say so and leave the gap open.
   Why: an invented detail that fits looks identical to a recovered one.
5. Evidence before conclusion. Definition before use.
   Why: readers follow the order you give them.
6. Give every rule and every claim its reason.
   Why: a reason lets the next reader judge for themselves.
7. Date what you quote. Author rulings and the current text lead;
   older material is dated evidence.
   Why: numbers and decisions drift between sessions.
8. Keep voices apart. Quote the author exactly. Mark your own readings
   [READING] and anything for the author to check [AUTHOR-VERIFY].
   Why: the finished work speaks in the author's voice.
9. Report a stumble as: location, what tripped you, type
   (anticipated, unanticipated, misread), urgency.
   Why: where you trip, the next reader trips too.
10. Before the session ends, write a capture: decided, learned, open.
    Mark each line [VERIFIED], [AUTHOR] or [READER], and carry each open
    line to the backlog.
    Why: what lives only in conversation ends with the session, and an open
    point left in a capture fades with it.

## On finishing

Write `genesis/captures/<date>-<topic>.md` from `genesis/captureTemplate.md`,
and tell the author it is there. In a session limited to chat, give the
capture in your final reply so the author can save it.
