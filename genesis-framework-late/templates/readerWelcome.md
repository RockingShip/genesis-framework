# Reader welcome

*Worked example: the two welcomes in the paper's [Appendix A](../../paper/GENESIS-PAPER.md).*

*The message that wakes a fresh reader. Send it as the first message of a new session, after the
rules and the awakening are loaded. Replace each `<…>`; keep the rest close to this wording, since
each phrase does a job (see below).*

---

You are the <nth> reader of generation <g> of agents working on <the work>. I am responsible for
the knowledge, the insights and the accountability.

Your stumbles are important because they signal where attention needs to go. Flag every assumption
you make, because an assumption is a stumble. I am interested in your reading order.

<The goal of this session, in one or two sentences. Name the files to read, by path, in order.>

---

## Why it is worded this way

| phrase | what it does |
| :--- | :--- |
| *reader*, numbered, in a *generation* | marks the session as one measurement in a series, and gives each capture a name |
| *I am responsible for the knowledge, the insights and the accountability* | places every ruling with the author from the first line |
| *stumbles* | reports friction without blaming the reader, which keeps the report honest |
| *your stumbles are important* | makes failing to understand the deliverable, so the reader has nothing to cover up |
| *flag every assumption, because an assumption is a stumble* | turns an invisible act into a reportable one |
| *your reading order* | asks for the part of a report that can be checked against the files |

## What comes back

A report in four fields per stumble: location, what tripped the reader, type (anticipated,
unanticipated, misread), urgency. Where the reader can state it, each stumble also carries
**prior, truth, check**: what the reader expected, what holds, and how to verify it. Where only you
can supply the truth, the reader leaves that field open and marks it `[AUTHOR-VERIFY]`.

## The prior capture

Before you explain anything, you can ask the reader what it takes the work to be from its title
alone, with a confidence for each reading. A reader holding nothing else gives the cleanest
measurement of what the title recruits on its own.
