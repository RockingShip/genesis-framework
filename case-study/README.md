# Case study: Topological Computing

The framework was developed on one manuscript: *Topological Computing*, by xyzzy of RockingShip
Research. This page says what that work is, what the framework did there, and where to find more.

## The work

Thirty years of research by a single researcher, implemented in C99, with public source and
reproducible telemetry. In the author's own summary:

> logic held as structure rather than function, as wiring rather than calculation. Boolean turns out
> to be composite over a single operator, and expression trees can be built from seamless
> interlocking prime components. Solving a system then returns the formula that generates every
> solution rather than computing one answer. Adjacent to logic synthesis and formal verification;
> open source; first paper near submission.

The manuscript runs to three volumes, theory, developer and operations, and at revision r47 holds
95,635 words in its volumes and 29,855 more in front matter and reference. Its central teaching
problem made it a hard test for AI co-authoring: its vocabulary collides with existing fields, so a
reader, human or model, maps each new construct onto the nearest familiar one and reports that it
understands.

## What the framework did there

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Sessions** | well over a hundred, from 2025 onwards, across two model eras |
| **Frameworks** | the co-creative framework 3.1 to 8.2.3 for early and middle stages, then v9 for the late stage; see [`genesis-framework-early`](../genesis-framework-early/), [`genesis-framework-late`](../genesis-framework-late/) and the [lineage](LINEAGE.md) |
| **Readers** | six generations of fresh readers, each reporting where it stumbled; the sixth began on 2026-10-11 |
| **Findings** | about 139 recorded findings by the fourth generation, every one in the account and none in the architecture |
| **Corrections** | twenty onboarding corrections at r47, each written as prior, truth and check; four are marked as made independently by four readers |
| **Register** | the stock phrasings of unsteered model output count zero across the manuscript at r47, apart from five uses of *furthermore* |

The [paper](../paper/GENESIS-PAPER.md) gives the account in full: the instruments, the
incidents behind the rules, the measurements, and the four readers who tested v9 on its first day.
