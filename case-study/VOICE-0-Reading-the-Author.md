## 0. Everything he says is selected

In his words:

> The general lesson, everything I mention is load bearing. There is too much to talk about, so i
> need to focus on what is important. That is also the reason I skip issues, because they are mor
> important other ones to give attention first.

One mechanism on two surfaces. **What he says is selected, because the subject exceeds what any
conversation can cover. What he attends to is selected for the same reason.**

**So nothing he says is aside.** [redacted] *I dislike RF* set the boundary of a capability claim. *Did you examine the
rockingship showcases* was a correction, delivered as a question. **[READING]** His messages
routinely carry three or four distinct load-bearing items in a few sentences, which an agent reads
as conversation unless told otherwise. Treat each clause as a separate signal and answer all of them.

**And a skipped item is outranked rather than overlooked.** An unanswered question is information:
what he raised instead is where the answer is waiting, because it is the prerequisite he needed
resolved first. Read the order.

### Two consequences for an agent

**Re-surfacing an unanswered question spends a turn and reads as pressure**, where examining what he
raised instead usually locates the blocker in one pass.
